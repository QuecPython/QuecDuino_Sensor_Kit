**Buzzer and Its Principle**

### (1) Introduction to Buzzer

1. Function of Buzzer: A buzzer is an integrated electronic sounder powered by DC voltage, which is widely used as a sounding device in electronic products such as computers, printers, copiers, alarms, electronic toys, automotive electronic equipment, telephones, timers, etc.
2. Classification of Buzzer: Buzzers are mainly divided into piezoelectric buzzers and electromagnetic buzzers.
3. Circuit Graphic Symbol of Buzzer: The buzzer is represented by the letter "H" or "HA" (old standards use "FM", "LB", "JD", etc.) in the circuit.

### (2) Structural Principle of Buzzer

1. Piezoelectric Buzzer: The piezoelectric buzzer is mainly composed of a multivibrator, a piezoelectric buzzer piece, an impedance matcher, a resonance box, a shell, etc. Some piezoelectric buzzers are also equipped with light-emitting diodes on the shell. The multivibrator is composed of transistors or integrated circuits. When the power is turned on (1.5~15V DC working voltage), the multivibrator oscillates and outputs an audio signal of 1.5~2.5kHz, and the impedance matcher drives the piezoelectric buzzer piece to sound. The piezoelectric buzzer piece is made of piezoelectric ceramic materials such as lead zirconate titanate or lead niobate magnesium. Silver electrodes are plated on both sides of the ceramic piece, and after polarization and aging treatment, it is bonded with a brass piece or stainless steel piece.
2. Electromagnetic Buzzer: The electromagnetic buzzer is composed of an oscillator, an electromagnetic coil, a magnet, a vibrating diaphragm, a shell, etc. When the power is turned on, the audio signal current generated by the oscillator passes through the electromagnetic coil, making the electromagnetic coil generate a magnetic field. Under the interaction of the electromagnetic coil and the magnet, the vibrating diaphragm vibrates periodically to produce sound.

**What is the difference between active buzzer and passive buzzer?**

The "source" here does not refer to the power source, but the oscillation source. That is to say, the active buzzer has an internal oscillation source, so it will sound as soon as it is powered on. The passive buzzer has no internal oscillation source, so it cannot be made to sound with a DC signal. It must be driven by a square wave of 2K~5K. Active buzzers are often more expensive than passive ones because they have an additional oscillation circuit inside.

Advantages of passive buzzer: 1. Cheap; 2. Controllable sound frequency, can produce the effect of "Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si"; 3. In some special cases, it can share a control port with LED.

Advantage of active buzzer: Easy program control.
